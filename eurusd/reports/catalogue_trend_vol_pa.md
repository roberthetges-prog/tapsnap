# Catalogue test: breakout/trend (T01-T18), volatility (V01-V04) and price action (P01-P12)

Engine: `eurusd.backtest` at commit 4fe754c (limit fills cannot take profit on the entry bar unless M1 proves it; market/stop orders may). All families were (re)run on that engine. Costs 1.0 pip spread + 0.2 pip slippage per fill. TRAIN 2003-2015 (grid, best by avg_R x sqrt(trades) among points with >= 50 trades), VALIDATE 2016-2022 once with the chosen parameters. HOLDOUT untouched. Survival: avg R > 0, t >= 2, PF >= 1.15, >= 60 % years positive, >= 30 trades, neighbourhood median > 0 on TRAIN.

Code: `src/eurusd/strategies_trend_pa.py` (29 strategies registered), driver `experiments/12_trend_vol_pa.py`, full numbers `experiments/results/trend_vol_pa.csv`, per-family grids `experiments/results/tvp_<family>_train.csv`, logs with sample trades `experiments/results/logs/tvp_*.log`.

## Two engine artefacts found and neutralised in this module (they matter for other families too)

1. **Pending orders are evaluated one by one over their whole validity window, not chronologically.** For an OCO buy-stop/sell-stop pair the long side is always taken when it fills anywhere in the window, even if the sell level was hit first; since the long's stop usually sits at the sell level, a certain -1R day becomes a fresh trade. `resolve_pending()` re-anchors every stop/limit order at the bar before its first touch (1-bar validity, identical fill) and keeps only the first-touched side of a pair (same-bar tie broken with M1, dropped if still tied). Effect: T15 Larry Williams M5 went from train t=+10.6 / validate t=+5.7 (a fake 'survivor') to t=+0.2 / -1.0. The lead's `asian_range_breakout` / `opening_range_breakout` families (S01 'arb_m15' validate t=2.25, 'orb_london_m5' train t=7.3) place the same OCO pairs and should be re-checked with this resolution.

2. **Limit fill + same-bar target** (now fixed in the engine): order blocks H1 had train t=+5.9 raw, t=-2.0 with M1 resolution; FVG H1 +4.0 -> -1.2; supply/demand H4 +2.7 -> +0.3. Limit families here are run with `ltf=M1`.

## Not implemented / caveats

* V05/V06 (NFP/CPI events) are out of scope here (event calendar collected by another agent).
* T01 'skip the signal after a winning S1 trade' needs the fill history inside the strategy function; omitted (T02 has no skip rule anyway).
* T17 catalogue version sizes positions to a vol target and nets 1/3/12-month signals; the engine trades one unit, so the engine rows use sign-only signals and the vol-scaled version is reported separately from daily closes (no engine).
* V03: GARCH(1,1) (not MS-GARCH); the 3-regime split uses the trailing-500-day tercile of the one-day-ahead sigma forecast; the entropy filter needs regime probabilities and was not built.
* V04: no EUR/USD 1-month implied-vol history in data/; VIX used as the risk proxy (fred_VIXCLS.csv).
* P12: tick volume is zero in the data, so the 'expanding volume' condition is dropped.
* P05/P07: mechanical pivot patterns are rare (10-170 trades in 13 years on H4/D1); their validate samples are too small to say much either way.
* Flip-exit systems (T03/T07/T11/T12/T13/T17): a stop-and-reverse on the exit bar is blocked by the engine's one-position rule, so the reverse entry is re-issued one bar later.
* Trailing/chandelier exits (T14) are filled at the close of the bar on which the trail is touched (engine supports only fixed stops), a direction-neutral approximation.
